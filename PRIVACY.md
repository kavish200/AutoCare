# Privacy Policy for AutoCare

_Last updated: September 2026_

AutoCare is a personal/development project. This privacy policy is provided to comply with Google OAuth requirements for development and testing purposes.

## Information We Collect

AutoCare may collect the following information when you use the application:
- Email address (for account creation, login, and verification)
- Name (if provided during registration)
- Basic usage data necessary for app functionality (e.g., vehicle service records, appointment details)

## How We Use Your Information

Collected information is used solely to:
- Authenticate and manage your account
- Send transactional emails (e.g., verification, password reset)
- Provide core AutoCare functionality

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database and is not shared publicly.

## Third-Party Services

AutoCare uses Google OAuth2 solely for sending transactional emails via Gmail. We do not use this access to read, store, or share your Gmail data beyond what is required for sending application emails.
